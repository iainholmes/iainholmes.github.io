# IH. personal signature

These are the approved personal artwork, shared by the publications hosted in
this repository. They identify the person behind the work; they do not replace
publication mastheads, application branding, or navigation.

- Canonical master: `desk/assets/identity/ih-master.png`, the unchanged approved
  1254 × 1254 PNG. It is referenced for provenance, not copied or modified here.
- `ih-signature.png`: unchanged 466 × 438 crop of that master, bounds
  `(400, 380, 866, 818)`. The full IH. artwork, including the illustrated period.
- `ih-period.png`: unchanged 60 × 60 crop, bounds `(780, 723, 840, 783)`.
  Rupert and the loblolly remain part of the artwork.

Master SHA-256:
`7e2dabb7244b44c8a582792f59ff3cb1fa0eb8e911a8a4e7c249d68c5ba0c619`

Both crops are byte-identical copies of the approved identity assets in
`desk/assets/identity/`. Do not redraw, filter, recolor, distort, or remove their paper
ground. Dark publications accommodate that ground rather than alter the logo.
The master is not downloaded by these pages.

The shared footer uses the full mark at 44 CSS pixels wide, with its original
aspect ratio. The Atlas masthead uses the separate period at 24 CSS pixels.
These are discreet identification sizes: the illustrated period in the full
footer mark is too small to resolve as a detailed dog/tree drawing, but its
original pixels are retained without a simplified substitute. Use the master
when detailed inspection of that illustration is required.

`identity.css` scopes all additions to `ih-*` classes and inherits host type and
color. Periodicals pages use “Periodicals | IH.”, preserving publication-specific
closing elements and links. Field Brief's edition render template and index,
Loblolly & Logit's archive/register dialogs, and all Atlas pages generated by
`chrome()` include the signature. Future editions inherit those same templates.

The personal website places the mark beside copyright. The Atlas retains its
masthead, date line, navigation, and existing Rupert portrait; its footer reads
“THE RUPERT ATLAS | IH.”. Econometric Advisor and QST use separate review PRs in
their own repositories.

Validation: `python scripts/test_identity_rollout.py`, `node scripts/test_identity_runtime.cjs`, the existing Periodicals
source/runtime suites, and `npm run check && npm test` in `rupert/`.
Full desktop/iPhone browser visual acceptance remains required before release.
