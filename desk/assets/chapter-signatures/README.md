# IH. collection — companion editorial signatures

Three original, hand-authored SVG devices for the accepted collection presentation. These are secondary chapter identities, not replacement IH. logos or representations of application functionality. The canonical personal artwork is never traced, recolored, embedded in these vectors or imitated with substitute initials.

| Device | Concept | Copper relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `inquiry.svg` | A divider construction above concentric drafting arcs, registration ticks and a measured baseline. | The instrument's pivot. |
| `field-folio.svg` | One continuous idea: an open scholarly folio whose spine extends into a pine sprig. No collage of separate destination icons. | A quiet impression beneath the book's spine. |
| `scholar.svg` | An abstract pediment, columns and arched opening, bounded by the corners of a bookplate. It depicts no named or identifiable building. | The center of the architectural lintel. |

The family shares a 160 × 120 viewBox, forest/ivory inherited ink, fine rounded strokes, open space and fixed copper `#976144`. Drawing silhouettes differ deliberately. Dunhill Oblique chapter labels are ordinary HTML text beside the vectors, preserving the site's authentic typography and text scaling. There are no fonts, scripts, external resources or embedded images inside the SVGs.

Above 800px, each signature sits at the right of its chapter heading: 72 × 54px artwork and an 18px label. At 800px and below, it forms a compact 48 × 36px running signature beside the chapter number, above the full-width title; its label is 17px. This changes the arrangement rather than crowding an icon into a mobile title. Do not shrink the artwork below that mobile size.

Any reveal within a chapter hides its companion signature using the existing `data-active` state, including individual application reveals. Returning the chapter to its full collection restores the signature. No new script, navigation or animation is introduced. The signatures and labels are decorative (`aria-hidden`); chapter headings remain the accessible names, and no signature receives pointer or keyboard input.

It includes the separately framed, unchanged canonical logo for comparison. It is an asset/composition proof, **not an integrated browser screenshot**. Physical Mac/iPhone review remains the final layout gate.
