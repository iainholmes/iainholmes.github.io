Reviewed access/trail descriptions do not establish the missing crowd ratings or quiet windows. This does not prove that no applicable research exists anywhere. No historical crowd values are populated from these sources.

The concrete presentation defect was redundant unknowns: two empty metrics plus a separate no-data message. Entirely unknown records now show one concise statement specific to the edition. Partial records show only documented readings/facts. Existing filtering still treats unknown as unknown, never quiet. No safety or publication gate changes.

Prospective instructions encourage attributable qualitative guidance found during ordinary research, with source/location/period/limits in existing fields. Annual park totals, review counts and anecdotes must not become trail predictions. Enrichment is optional, with no new mandatory research gate. An existing integrity test incorrectly required every future snapshot to remain null; it now tests honest rendering instead, allowing genuine future evidence without modifying historical data.
