Tuesday's choiceFor the weekend of Sat 3 – Sun 4 October

Cox Mountain and the Fews Ford crossing

A 3.75-mile loop that crosses the Eno on a suspension bridge, climbs about 300 feet to the ridge, and comes back down to the water.

Why this week

Saturday morning is the better half of the weekend. Friday runs near 90°F; Saturday drops to a high near 78 with a 30% chance of showers, and Sunday is wetter. Go early Saturday and the climb happens in the cool.

Conditions

Friday near 90°F. Saturday partly sunny, high near 78, 30% chance of showers. Sunday mostly cloudy, high near 75, 40%.

If it turns

Showers set in before 9 a.m. Saturday: Switch to Carolina North and save Cox Mountain for a dry morning; the ridge rock is slick when wet. Go to: Carolina North Forest
