Thursday's ChoiceFor the weekend of Sat 10 – Sun 11 October

Raven Rock and the Stairs Down to the Cape Fear

A 2.6-mile loop through pine woods to a 150-foot cliff above the river, with stairs down to its foot and an overlook above.

Thursday edition · 8 Oct · Weekend choice 2 of 2

Why This Week

The longer-drive choice, only if Saturday morning remains dry and the park is open. The loop and cliff are the outing; the long stairs make wet footing a reason to turn back. October opening is 7 a.m. Sunday thunderstorms are a reason to postpone, not to choose another outdoor trail.

Conditions

NWS forecast issued 8 October: Saturday has a 50% chance of rain after 8 a.m., high near 72°F. Saturday night has a 90% chance of rain and thunderstorms, with three quarters to one inch possible. Sunday: showers and thunderstorms, 90%, high near 79°F. This is a conditional dry-Saturday outing, not an all-weather recommendation.

If it turns

Rain, thunder, flooding or an official closure: Postpone the outing. Another outdoor trail is not a safe thunderstorm fallback. Recheck the official park status and NWS forecast before leaving.
