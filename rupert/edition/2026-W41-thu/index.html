Thursday's ChoiceFor the weekend of Sat 10 – Sun 11 October

Raven Rock and the Stairs Down to the Cape Fear

A 2.6-mile loop through pine woods to a 150-foot cliff above the river, with stairs down to its foot and an overlook above.

Published Thu 8 Oct · 07:00 ET · Weekend choice 2 of 2

Why This Week

The longer-drive choice, for a day with nothing else in it. Rupert gets the river at the bottom of the stairs; you get the cliff. The park opens at 7 a.m. in October, so an early start beats the Saturday crowd at the rock.

Conditions

Outlook only, well ahead of the weekend: no clear rain signal, and mid-October highs are typically in the low to mid 70s. This will be replaced by the forecast on publication day.

If it turns

Heavy rain in the two days before: The paths along the Cape Fear can flood after heavy rain. Take the Company Mill loop instead, where the creek is crossed on a bridge. Go to: Umstead, Company Mill the long way
