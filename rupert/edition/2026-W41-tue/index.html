Tuesday's choiceFor the weekend of Sat 10 – Sun 11 October

Occoneechee Mountain and the quarry overlook

A 2.2-mile loop that climbs about 350 feet above the Eno to a rocky overlook at the old quarry, then drops back to the river.

Why this week

The short-and-close choice: twenty minutes out, done well before lunch, with a real climb and a view at the top. The outlook for the weekend has no clear signal either way, so this is the plan that survives a changing forecast. If Saturday looks wet on Friday night, go on Sunday instead.

Conditions

Outlook only, well ahead of the weekend: no clear rain signal, and mid-October highs are typically in the low to mid 70s. This will be replaced by the forecast on publication day.

If it turns

Rain or thunder on Saturday morning: Brumley is ten minutes away. Save the overlook for a clear day, when the rock is dry. Go to: Brumley Forest

Forecast as of Mon 28 Sep, 15:00 ET

Rupert's Mission

Reach the overlook, then the river, and see which one Rupert chooses to sit at.
