Thursday's ChoiceFor the weekend of Sat 24 – Sun 25 October

A Small Loop Among the Longleaf Pines

One easy mile at Weymouth Woods: tall longleaf pines, sandy ground and a generous pace, with a shorter loop beside the parking if that is enough for Rupert.

Thursday edition · 22 Oct · Weekend choice 2 of 2

The Outing

Weymouth Woods
Visitor-center parking, 1024 Fort Bragg Road, Southern Pines

easy

Why This Week

After Tuesday’s longer oak-hickory and wetland circuit, this is a different scale and landscape: one unhurried mile through the open longleaf pines of the main tract. Follow the blue-triangle Pine Barrens loop from visitor-center parking. Wildlife sightings are a possibility, not a promise. Allow an hour to an hour and a half for the walk and Rupert’s pauses. The western Equestrian Trail closure is separate from this route; do not extend onto that trail or assume the Boyd and Paint Hill tracts share this access. The publication-day NWS forecast is sunny and cool, with a northeast breeze on Saturday.

Conditions

Saturday: Sunny, with a high near 66. Northeast wind 7 to 13 mph. Sunday: Sunny, with a high near 68. Saturday night: Clear, with a low around 45. Sunday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 43.

If it turns

Thunder, strong wind, flooding, unsafe footing or an official closure: Postpone rather than substituting another exposed outing. Recheck official access and the current NWS forecast before leaving.

A Short Main-tract Loop

The official Pine Barrens Trail is one mile, easy, and marked with blue triangles. Begin at visitor-center parking; the separate tracts are not part of this plan.

Keep the Closure Separate

The western Equestrian Trail has a maintenance closure. The suggested hiking loop does not use that segment; obey all on-site signs and recheck the status before leaving.
