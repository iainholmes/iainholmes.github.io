Great Blue Heron, at a Labrador’s Pace

A woodland circuit and wetland edge at Iron Ore Belt: roughly four miles with the approach, a picnic if it suits, and time for Rupert to read the ground.

Why This Week

After last week’s ridge and lakeshore, this is a different stretch of Piedmont woods: oak-hickory canopy, wetland margins and a loop with room for unhurried sniffing. Start at Iron Ore Belt, not The Summit. The official circuit is 3.2 miles; add the Connector Trail out and back for about 3.9 miles from the parking area. Allow two to three hours outside as a planning estimate, longer with a picnic. The publication-day forecast points to a sunny, cool weekend; take a layer for the morning and recheck the weather before leaving. There is no promise of seeing the bird in the trail’s name.

Conditions

Saturday: Sunny, with a high near 63. Sunday: Sunny, with a high near 68. Saturday night: Clear, with a low around 38. Sunday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 42.

If it turns

Thunder, strong wind, flooding, unsafe footing or an official closure: Postpone rather than substituting another exposed walk or picnic. Recheck official access and the current NWS forecast before leaving.

The Whole Distance

The Great Blue Heron loop is 3.2 miles, but parking-to-parking includes the Connector Trail twice. Plan on about 3.9–4 miles and allow two to three hours outside.

For Rupert

Keep the leash attended and no longer than six feet. Bring his drinking water, pause before the wetland edge, and shorten the walk if his pace or the footing calls for it.
