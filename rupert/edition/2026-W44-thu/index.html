Laurel, with Room for a Picnic

A short woodland loop and a leisurely picnic at Morrow Mountain: a deliberately gentle Uwharrie day, rather than a summit hike.

Why This Week

Tuesday’s farm circuit stays closer to the Sandhills; this western option makes a longer day of a much shorter walk. Follow the red-hexagon Laurel loop opposite the park office and museum, then leave time for a picnic in an open designated area. The official 0.7 mile is the complete loop, not the longer Morrow Mountain Trail. It is a fairly long drive for a small walk, so choose this for an unhurried Uwharrie visit rather than a mileage goal. Rupert stays outside the museum. Hiking trails are marked open; the park now asks walkers to report downed trees, so an open status is not a promise of obstacle-free footing. This morning’s NWS forecast is mostly cloudy around 70F for both weekend days; recheck it before leaving.

Conditions

Saturday: Mostly cloudy, with a high near 70. Northeast wind 5 to 8 mph. Sunday: Mostly cloudy, with a high near 70. Saturday night: Mostly cloudy, with a low around 48. Sunday night: A slight chance of rain showers after 1am. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 50.

If it turns

Thunder, strong wind, flooding, unsafe footing or an official closure: Postpone. Another exposed trail or a picnic is not a storm alternative; recheck official status and NWS before leaving.

The Shorter Woodland Choice

The official Laurel Trail is 0.7 mile, easy, and marked with red hexagons. Start at the park-office/museum area shown on the official map; this is not the 2.6-mile Morrow Mountain Trail.

Keep the Day Modest

Allow time for Rupert’s pauses and a picnic if conditions permit. Pets cannot enter the museum. Skip the outing for unsafe weather or footing rather than adding a harder trail.

Parking
Use designated parking at the park office/museum, then the signed Laurel Trail opposite this area on the official map. Do not park on road shoulders. Free day use. October gates 7 a.m.–9 p.m., November 7 a.m.–8 p.m.; day-use areas close one hour before the gate. Finish in daylight.

Dog Access

Leash
Attended leash no longer than six feet. Pets stay outside buildings, pool and vacation cabins.
Water
Carry drinking water and a bowl. No swimming is part of this outing.
Shade
Woodland shade on the walking loop; do not assume the parking or picnic area is shaded.
Watch for
Roots, uneven ground and slippery wet leaves; Keep Rupert close beside the park road; Wildlife, ticks and autumn trail debris
