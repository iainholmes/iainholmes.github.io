Alder, on the Smaller Lake

A compact woodland circuit around Park Lake, with a picnic rather than a long hike as the shape of the day.

Why This Week

Tuesday’s eastern woodland choice is a little longer on foot; this western alternative deliberately makes less of the walking and more of a leisurely picnic. Alder is an easy 0.8-mile loop beside Park Lake, not the longer Lake Shore circuit or Itusi bike network. Follow the white diamonds from the visitor-center area, leaving Rupert time to sniff. It is a long drive for a small walk, so choose it for an unhurried park day rather than a mileage goal. Bring all drinking water for both of you: an old official boil-water notice remains online and its rescission has not been verified. No boat rental, swimming or visitor-center entry for Rupert is part of the plan. The publication-day NWS forecast is sunny in the mid-60s for both weekend days, with colder nights. Treat that as a dated outlook and check again before the long drive.

Conditions

Saturday: Sunny, with a high near 66. North northwest wind around 5 mph. Sunday: Sunny, with a high near 65. Saturday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 38. Sunday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 39.

If it turns

Thunder,strong wind,flooding,unsafe footing or official closure: Postpone. Another exposed trail or picnic is not a storm alternative. Recheck official status and NWS before leaving.

Parking
Use designated visitor-center parking at 759 State Park Road and the signed Alder Trail by Park Lake, as shown on the official map. This is not the longer Lake Shore Trail or Itusi bike system. The November hours table closes the park at 6 p.m.; a separate hiking status panel still says 7:30 p.m. Use the earlier 6 p.m. limit and finish in daylight. Free ordinary day use.
