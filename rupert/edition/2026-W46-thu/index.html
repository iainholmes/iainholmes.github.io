A Two-mile Turn Over Mayo Mountain

A modest hardwood loop from the picnic shelter, with a shorter inner loop if Rupert has had enough.

Thursday edition · 12 Nov · Weekend choice 2 of 2

Why This Week

Start at the main Mayo Mountain access and take the blue two-mile loop through mixed hardwoods. The park describes a creek and seasonal views of Cedar Mountain; neither calls for leaving the trail. Keep the red half-mile inner loop in reserve for a shorter walk, and leave the separate waterfall and river accesses for another day. Finish in daylight and heed the earlier access closing time. The dated forecast puts both days in the low 50s after cold nights. A late-morning start gives the shaded path some time to warm; wet leaves can still hide slick roots.

Conditions

Saturday: Sunny, with a high near 51. Northwest wind 2 to 12 mph. Sunday: Sunny, with a high near 54. Saturday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 28. Sunday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 27.

If it turns

Thunder, high water, unsafe footing, strong wind or official closure: Postpone and recheck official park status and NWS. Do not substitute another exposed outing.
