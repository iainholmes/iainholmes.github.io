Under the Laurels at Ivy Bluff

Follow the Yadkin’s south bank on a short, rooty out-and-back with no obligation to cross a swollen creek.

Why This Week

At Ivy Bluff, mountain laurel and rhododendron frame the south bank of the Yadkin. The red circles lead over sloping ground and across creeks; 1.3 miles out is enough for a 2.6-mile return. Let Rupert sniff on a short leash away from the water’s edge. Turn before any crossing that looks slick or high, and skip the trip if the park posts a closure or the river is rising. Saturday has the better window in the dated forecast. Early Sunday showers are possible, so check the creek approaches and be ready to shorten the walk.

If it turns

Thunder, unsafe footing, high water, strong winds or park closure: Postpone and recheck the official forecast and park status. Do not substitute an exposed outdoor outing.
