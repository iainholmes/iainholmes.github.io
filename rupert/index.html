Occoneechee Mountain and the Quarry Overlook

A 2.2-mile loop that climbs about 350 feet above the Eno to a rocky overlook at the old quarry, then drops back to the river.

Outing at a glance

  • 2.2 mi loop
  • 20–25 min drive
  • 1–1½ h outside

Raven Rock and the Stairs Down to the Cape Fear

A 2.6-mile loop through pine woods to a 150-foot cliff above the river, with stairs down to its foot and an overlook above.

Outing at a glance

  • 2.6 mi loop
  • 1 h 10 – 1 h 20 drive
  • 2–3 h outside

Weather warning

DayForecast
SaturdayA chance of rain after 8am. Cloudy, with a high near 72. Northeast wind 7 to 14 mph, with gusts as high as 26 mph. Chance of precipitation is 50%. New rainfall amounts between a tenth and quarter of an inch possible.
SundayShowers and thunderstorms. Cloudy, with a high near 79. Chance of precipitation is 90%.

NWS forecast issued 8 October: Saturday has a 50% chance of rain after 8 a.m., high near 72°F. Saturday night has a 90% chance of rain and thunderstorms, with three quarters to one inch possible. Sunday: showers and thunderstorms, 90%, high near 79°F. This is a conditional dry-Saturday outing, not an all-weather recommendation.

Forecast snapshot · issued Thu 8 Oct, 16:48 ET · National Weather Service

Trail note

Dry Saturday only. Wet stairs, flooded paths or thunder: turn back or postpone. Stay on marked trails and out of the Cape Fear.
