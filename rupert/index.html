The Long Way Around Jones Lake

A four-mile circuit through Carolina bay forest, with pond cypress, boardwalks and tea-coloured water rather than a summit to reach.

  • 2 h 25 – 2 h 45 drive

Trail note

Allow for roots, boggy soil, and frost or moisture on shaded boards. Stay on the marked trail; turn back rather than walking around a flooded or slippery section. Carry water and finish in daylight.
