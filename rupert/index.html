Hanging Rock, at Rupert’s Pace

A 3.6-mile round trip from the visitor-center lot to the quartzite outcrop, with a wooded climb, stairs and room to turn back before the top.

Outing at a glance

  • 3.6 mi out and back
  • 2 h 15 – 2 h 30 drive
  • 2–3 h outside

Forecast snapshot · issued Tue 13 Oct, 15:00 ET · National Weather Service — current point forecast

Trail note

Follow the orange circles and return the same way. Keep Rupert on a six-foot leash and at least one body length from every cliff edge. Do not climb unstable edge rocks. Stop before stairs become uncomfortable.
