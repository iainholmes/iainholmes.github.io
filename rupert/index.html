Long Valley Farm, Beyond the Field Edge

An easy two-mile loop from old farm fields into longleaf pines, with an optional millpond detour and plenty of room in the plan for Rupert’s pauses.

Trail note

Stay on the marked Long Valley Farm loop and give bicycles room. Carry water even in late October; wet sand, roots and leaves may be slippery. The millpond is scenery, not a swimming stop.
